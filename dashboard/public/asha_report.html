📖 How to Use This App
Step 1 — Search or select your district (82 NE India districts).
Step 2 — Tap + to add each symptom case.
Step 3 — Move sliders to match water readings.
Step 4 — Press Submit Report.
No Internet? Use SMS Mode tab.
